# Enterprise AI Governance: Demo Walkthrough

Runtime 1:38. AI processing shown at 10x; everything else is real time.

**[0:00]** This is the Enterprise AI Governance system: every AI system in the company on one tamper-evident record, assessed by agents against a swappable rulebook, with humans owning every verdict.

**[0:07]** The control tower. One hundred ninety AI systems on record, tiered by risk under the live policy pack, with real measured numbers on every tile: open findings, overdue remediations, systems missing an owner.

**[0:17]** Watch a brand new AI system come under governance.

**[0:19]** You can fill a structured form, or just describe the system in plain words and let an agent do the paperwork.

**[0:33]** One click. An agent reads that paragraph, fills the registry record field by field, and then the full assessment pipeline runs against the active rulebook.

**[0:36]** Seconds later, at 10 times speed, CreditLens is on the estate.

**[0:36]** And on its record page the assessment narrates itself while it runs: which rulebooks apply, what tier the framework puts it in, what each inspector is checking.

**[0:40]** A few minutes of agent work, sped up 10 times. A credit scorer reading age and postcode lands exactly where it should: high risk, with findings.

**[0:47]** Each finding names the control it violates, the inspector that raised it, the evidence, and a concrete fix.

**[0:54]** Routing is agent work too: it reads the finding, picks the right desk, sets an owner and a deadline from severity.

**[0:56]** Routed, and the routing itself is on the audit chain.

**[1:03]** Humans are first-class here as well. A pen test result or an incident gets logged directly.

**[1:08]** It joins the same chain, attributed to the person who raised it, and lands on the remediation board like any agent finding.

**[1:11]** And underneath, the audit trail: every pipeline step, every finding, every human action, hash-chained. Tampering breaks the chain visibly.

**[1:24]** Every open finding across the whole estate lands on the remediation board with an owner, a deadline and a status. Nothing disappears into a spreadsheet.

**[1:29]** Overdue items are one click away, which is what makes the governance real rather than decorative.

**[1:35]** That is the loop: agents assess and route, people decide and remediate, and the record proves all of it.
